The studio

Made by one person, in India.

Rainhero Games is not a company with a floor of desks. It is one developer who writes the code, draws the art, picks the music and answers the support email.

That means fewer games, and more time spent on each one. The goal is simple: something you can start in ten seconds, understand in thirty, and still want to open tomorrow.

No forced sign-ups, no paywalls in the middle of a level. If a game is not fun without spending, it is not finished.
